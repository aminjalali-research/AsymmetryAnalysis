# Left–right asymmetry and normative-deviation methods for EZ/SOZ lateralization, 2023–2026

Prepared 2026-11-03 for the AsymmetryAnalysis project (ASL perfusion asymmetry, drug-resistant focal epilepsy).
Scope: perfusion (ASL), metabolic (FDG-PET), SPECT/SISCOM, structural MRI asymmetry, multimodal/ML, and
normative-modelling / z-map methodology applied to single-subject epilepsy imaging.

**Verification.** Every paper below was retrieved from PubMed (mcp__pubmed search + get_article_metadata; full
text via PMC where cited). PMID, DOI, exact title, journal, volume and year were taken from the PubMed record,
not from memory. Papers not findable on PubMed were excluded. Two medRxiv preprints are included only because
PubMed indexes them; they are labelled PREPRINT and are not peer-reviewed. Aslam 2022 is outside the window and
is included only as the PASCOM baseline. Numbers come from abstracts unless "(full text)" is stated.

Abbreviations: HC = healthy controls; HV = healthy volunteers; SF = seizure-free; RZ = resection zone;
SOZ = seizure-onset zone; AI = asymmetry index; LI = laterality index; CBM = cortex-based (surface) mapping;
SPM = statistical parametric mapping; MDT = multidisciplinary team; sens/spec = sensitivity/specificity;
ext = external validation on an independent cohort.

**Gaps in the 2023–2026 literature (relevant to the manuscript's framing).**
1. No peer-reviewed ASL study reports BOTH sensitivity and specificity for single-subject EZ lateralization against SEEG/outcome with a control false-positive check; A8 reports concordance only, A9/A11/A12 group statistics, A6/A7 DICE overlap.
2. No ASL study uses a symmetric template, normative voxel AI z-scores or leave-one-out control calibration; those elements come from PET (E15, B3) and structural (D1) work.
3. Localization below a lobe prior is not evaluated anywhere for ASL; B1 is the only paper giving lobe-wise sens/spec (temporal worst).
4. Interictal perfusion/metabolism lateralization has a low confident-call rate everywhere it is measured (C6: 4.5% high-confidence interictal SPECT; B1: temporal sens 0.41), so our 14% call rate at 6% FP is in line with the field, not an outlier.
